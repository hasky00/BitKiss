What BitKiss is

A wallet that is really yours. BitKiss creates 12 recovery words on your iPhone and keeps them there. Hasky Labs never sees them and cannot touch your sats.

Lightning, on your phone. BitKiss runs its own Lightning node inside the app, so payments settle in seconds and go straight from you to the receiver.

Bitcoin and Lightning together. Receive on-chain, send over Lightning, see both balances on one screen. Nothing else to set up, nothing to sign up for.

BitKiss runs on Bitcoin mainnet today. No app store required.
